# Tensor operations:
Tensor operations are the foundation of deep learning: a tensor is a multi-dimensional array whose shape must satisfy each operation's contract (matmul needs inner dims equal, broadcasting aligns from the right with 1s stretching), and most bugs come from shape mismatches that either crash or silently produce garbage. Build a Tensor class with flat storage + strides to understand reshape, transpose, and non-contiguity, then master broadcasting and einsum (where axes in inputs but not outputs get summed) as the universal tools. The payoff is tracing multi-head attention end-to-end — projection, head split via reshape+transpose, scores via bhtd,bhsd->bhts, softmax, weighted sum, head merge, output projection — so every shape is known at every step and errors become trivially debuggable.
