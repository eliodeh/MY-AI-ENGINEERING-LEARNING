# Complex numbers for AI
A complex number z = a + bi is just a 2D point/vector. The i tags the vertical axis. Multiplying by i = rotate 90° counterclockwise. That's why i² = -1 (two 90° turns = 180° = facing backward). Complex numbers are the natural language of rotations and waves.

i is a 90° rotation operator, e^(iθ) is rotation by θ, complex multiplication is 2D rotation+scale, and the DFT is just measuring how much a signal resembles rotating phasors.


# the arithematics
1. Add: add real parts, add imaginary parts → vector addition.

2. Multiply: FOIL, replace i² with -1 → rotate + scale.

3. Conjugate: flip imaginary sign → (a+bi)(a-bi) = a²+b² (always real).

4. Divide: multiply top & bottom by denominator's conjugate.
